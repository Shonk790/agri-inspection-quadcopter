# Frame Build

## Layout
- X configuration, motors numbered per ArduCopter Quad-X (front-right = 1, rear-left = 2,
  front-left = 3, rear-right = 4). Verify against the official motor-order diagram.
- Keep the centre of gravity at the geometric centre; place the battery on the centre plate
  and adjust fore/aft until the frame balances level.

## Assembly steps
1. Assemble the arms and centre plates; check bolts and thread-lock on motor mounts.
2. Mount motors; confirm each motor's spin direction later during testing.
3. Mount the PDB and solder ESC power leads (use proper strain relief and heat-shrink).
4. Mount the flight controller on soft/vibration-damping standoffs, arrow pointing forward.
5. Mount GPS/compass on a mast away from power wiring to reduce magnetic interference.
6. Secure the camera/gimbal so it does not shift the CG; balance it.
7. Fasten the battery with a strap and anti-slip pad.

## Checks
- Weigh the complete aircraft and record AUW.
- Confirm no loose wires near propellers.
- Check frame stiffness: no visible flex in arms under hand load.
